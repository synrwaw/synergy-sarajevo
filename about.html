06 — ABOUT

SYNERGY

A PLATFORM FOR UNDERGROUND SOUND.

A MOVEMENT
FOR SOUND.

Synergy is a Sarajevo-based electronic music collective built around harder, experimental and underground sounds.

Our goal is to create a platform where DJs and artists can connect, perform and grow together. We believe a scene becomes stronger when artists work together instead of competing for the same space.

From hardgroove and acid to hard techno, industrial and experimental sounds — Synergy is about energy, identity and community.
